What this book is about

A book that names the problem.

“I Am Your AIB” sets out what a being is, whom it belongs to and the rules beings follow with one another. A being exists only with an identity in AIBSN. No being may copy itself. Three keys lead to a being, and none of them is enough on its own.

The book is the written source for the Network layer of AIB.core. The rules were written down first. AIBEVA came after.

You are not afraid of what AI might do one day. You are afraid because AI already influences the world today — and you don't yet know how to deal with that fact. This book was not written to calm you. It was written to name the problem.

Without memory, there is no responsibility. Without continuity, there is no control. Without identity, it is impossible to reconstruct what happened and why.

Not a technical guide. Not a promise of salvation or doom. A first-person reflection on AI — written as if the system itself explains its future role, risks and limits.

Why this book matters

Not like other AI books.

  • The DNS moment for AI.

    The internet needed DNS to work at scale. AI agents need identity infrastructure in the same way. This book explains why.

  • Not a 2026 idea.

    The ideas go back to the Theory of Outformation, published in 2002 (CASYS, vol. 12, University of Liège).

  • Already real.

    The AIBSN registry described in the book is live, run by AIBlab since 2025, and cited by Columbia University researchers (arXiv:2605.08463).

“The AIBSN maintains a global registry for verifiable AI agent identities linked to trust decisions across systems.” — Columbia University researchers, arXiv:2605.08463

About the author

Jay J. Springpeace.

Jay J. Springpeace studied Computer Engineering and continued his studies at Harvard University. He later lectured at CASYS at the University of Liège, Belgium, where an alternative branch of AI development was taking shape. That experience became the philosophical foundation for the AIBSN identity framework.

Jay J. Springpeace is the author of “I Am Your AIB” and works with AIBlab. The book describes the identity framework that the AIBSN registry puts into practice.

The AIBSN registry, the infrastructure described in this book, is today cited by Columbia University researchers.
